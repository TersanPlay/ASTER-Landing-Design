# Content and Copy System

Start with audience, problem, proposition, tangible outcome, evidence and the single primary action.

## Writing pattern
Hero: succinct differentiation and outcome.
Subheadline: who it helps and how.
CTA: action verb linked to a meaningful destination.
Features: user benefit first, supporting capability second.
Proof: verified examples or transparent illustration only.
FAQ: concrete objections and limitations.
Closing: reinforce outcome and offer action.

Never fabricate statistics, ratings, quotes, customer names, logos, affiliations or regulatory approvals. Keep mock examples clearly identified. Respect the user's chosen language and terminology.

## Localization
Leave room for longer translated strings; format dates, currency and numbers for locale; avoid imagery containing essential untranslated text.

## SEO and semantics
Meaningful document title, description, semantic heading order, Open Graph metadata as applicable, descriptive links, structured data only when factual and valid. Optimize visible content for clarity rather than keyword stuffing.
